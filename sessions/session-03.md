## Decisions

- The AI-assisted form is owned by Fabián.
- **The Trustless Work escrow is replaced by a vault.** An escrow releases money from one payer to one receiver against deliverables; here one borrower repays many investors in proportion to what each put in, and its contracts cannot express that — each of its milestones pays a single `receiver`. Its repository also has no licence file, so copying the code needs their permission first. It still fits the funding leg, where many pay and one receives.
- **The vault is a loan, not a share of the property.** The vault never computes interest: `expected_repayment` is fixed at creation and each payout falls out of a token balance over the supply frozen at settlement.
- Applications are stored in the backend (Supabase table `launchpad_applications`); the Launchpad form submits them and the admin reviews them.
- The admin compliance checklist is rule based, not generated by an LLM: each check shows the real text of the article it rests on, pulled from Neko Legal KB.
- Neko Legal KB runs on Google Cloud Run and redeploys on every merge to `main`.
