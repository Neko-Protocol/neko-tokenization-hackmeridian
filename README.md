# Neko Tokenization

Built at **HackMeridian 2026** (ONE16, Lisbon). Builder track: **Scale**. Thematic track: **Stellar Composed**.

Neko is a live investing app on Stellar mainnet where people in Latin America earn in dollars from $5. At HackMeridian we are building the tokenization side:

- **Launchpad:** a business that wants to tokenize an asset goes through KYB, an AI-assisted form, Neko's legal and compliance approval, and gets its token issued and launched, with no jargon.
- **Neko Pro:** verified investors find tokenized opportunities, from businesses that used the Launchpad or that already tokenize (for example Etherfuse), and invest from a minimum amount.

Tokenizing is still hard, and even when you manage it, you have no buyers. Neko solves both.
