# Neko tokenization roadmap beyond HackMeridian

For three to six months after the hackathon. This is the ideal direction for Launchpad and Neko's new investment opportunities, building on the existing Neko app.

## Where we want to go

Make Neko a place where businesses can bring real-world assets to market and LATAM investors can discover, invest in, and follow those opportunities in one experience.

Launchpad addresses the technology gap for businesses that want to tokenize. Neko connects those businesses and issuers that already tokenize with eligible investors. Together, they expand Neko from distributing existing assets into supporting the full journey from issuance to investment.

## What comes next

### Work with the first real issuers

Turn the hackathon experience into a service for a small group of LATAM businesses. Support both issuers starting from zero and those looking for distribution of existing tokens.

For new issuers, bring together onboarding, legal structuring with qualified partners, tokenization and fundraising. For existing issuers, focus on listing their opportunities and reaching suitable investors.

### Bring Launchpad opportunities into Neko

Build on Neko's existing discovery, asset detail, wallet, and portfolio experience. Giving eligible users access to new opportunities alongside assets from existing issuers.

Start with the existing web experience and extend access through Neko Mobile as it develops. Connect onboarding and verification across the experience wherever possible, so investing in a new opportunity feels like a natural next step for an existing Neko user.

### Grow distribution and partnerships

Use Neko's existing audience as the starting point for new offerings. Work with issuers on joint distribution, use Neko's public channels to explain opportunities, and build relationships with businesses seeking access to capital.

Validate pricing with early partners and investors before fixing the commercial model. Expand into more asset categories as demand and local requirements become clearer, with Costa Rica as an initial market to evaluate.

## What success looks like

By three to six months after the event, the aim is to have real issuer relationships, initial investment opportunities connected to Neko, and investors able to follow their holdings and payments. Those early experiences should establish how Neko can earn revenue and support more issuers without rebuilding the process for each one.
